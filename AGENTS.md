# Repository Guidelines

Shiftly: a web app for planning shift schedules with labour-law checks (planner and employee roles), built on the 10x Astro Starter: Astro 7 SSR, React 19 islands, Tailwind 4, Supabase auth, deployed to Cloudflare Workers.

## Hard Rules

- Product scope and domain rules live in `@context/foundation/prd.md`; the stack hand-off is `@context/foundation/tech-stack.md`. Do not invent labour-law thresholds or scheduling rules; they are open questions in the PRD.
- `context/archive/` is read-only by convention (`@context/archive/README.md`).
- `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_KEY` are server-only secrets (`@astro.config.mjs` env schema). Keep them in `.env` / `.dev.vars` (gitignored), never in code.
- `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` bypasses RLS. It is a Worker secret in production and an entry in the local `.dev.vars` (the key of the local stack only). Only `@src/lib/supabase-admin.ts` reads it, and only the invitation route `src/pages/api/employees/[id]/invite.ts` may import that module (ESLint enforces it). Do not use the key anywhere else, scripts excepted, and do not read data with it.
- Files use LF (`@.gitattributes`); Prettier reports every line of a CRLF file as an error.
- Packages with install scripts need approval (`npm install-scripts approve <pkg>`); approvals are pinned in `allowScripts` in `@package.json`.

## Project Structure

- `src/pages/` routes (auth API in `src/pages/api/auth/`, the public `src/pages/auth/set-password.astro` that an invitation link opens, planner pages in `src/pages/employees/`, `src/pages/shifts/` and `src/pages/schedules/` (its `[id]/assign.astro` is the view that puts employees on shifts), their JSON routes in `src/pages/api/employees/`, `src/pages/api/shifts/` and `src/pages/api/schedules/` (its `[id]/assignments.ts` adds and takes off one assignment), and the invitation route `src/pages/api/employees/[id]/invite.ts`), `src/layouts/PlannerLayout.astro` (frame of the planner pages; `wide` gives a table more room), `src/components/` (`ui/` shadcn, `auth/` sign-in and set-password forms, `employees/` employee form and invite button, `shifts/` shift form, `schedules/` schedule form with its staffing grid, employee picker and the D6 notice, and the assignment grid, `hooks/` React hooks), `src/lib/` helpers (`schedules.ts` reads and saves schedules, the rules are in `@src/lib/services/schedule.ts`; `assignments.ts` reads and writes assignments, the rules are in `@src/lib/services/assignment.ts`), `src/middleware.ts` access gate (`decideAccess` in `@src/lib/services/access.ts`, default deny, roles from `app_metadata`).
- `supabase/` config (public sign-up is off), the invitation mail templates in `supabase/templates/` and `migrations/` (the tables `employees`, `shifts`, `schedules`, `schedule_staffing`, `schedule_employees` and `schedule_assignments` (an employee on a shift on a day, with a `source` of `manual` or `generated`) with planner-only RLS, the function `save_schedule`, which saves a schedule (through the app) in one transaction as a diff that keeps assignments and needs a confirmation (`PT409` otherwise) to drop an assigned cell or person, and the employee accounts: `employees.activated_at`, the first password set from a link, is written only by the SQL function `public.mark_account_active()` that the set-password route calls with the employee's session), `scripts/` (`smoke.mjs` smoke test, `create-planner.mjs` first planner), `context/` planning documents.
- Live work-time rules (S-05): the assignment view lists critical violations and warnings under the grid, counted in the island after every click from the optimistic list of assignments (`@src/components/hooks/useViolations.ts`, `@src/components/schedules/ViolationList.tsx`, `@src/lib/services/violation-view.ts`); nothing is requested or stored. The rules are pure functions on whole minutes in `@src/lib/services/rules.ts` (`evaluateSchedule`), `@src/lib/services/work-hours.ts`, `@src/lib/services/calendar.ts` (computed Polish holidays, 2020 to 2099) and `@src/lib/services/fte.ts`. A `Violation` (`@src/types.ts`) has a stable `key`, `severity`, `rule`, `anchors`, `reason` and `actual` / `limit` / `unit`, and S-07, S-08 and S-09 build on it.
- Decisions of those rules: the weekly norm (40 h times the FTE, less 8 h times the FTE per holiday on a day other than Sunday) is a warning, not a critical violation, because the norm of the law is monthly; the day is the 24 hours from the start of a shift; the week of the 48 h and of the weekly rest is the seven days of the schedule, and the free time at its end and at its start counts as one break across the edge; a shift over 8 h is critical, with the sentence that the equivalent working-time system (art. 135) is not supported; only one schedule is judged and its edges (the previous and the next week) are not checked; Shiftly warns and does not guarantee legal compliance. The assignment island now receives each employee's FTE and each shift's start and end in minutes, but never an e-mail address.
- Hours against the norm (S-06): each person's row of the assignment view ends with a column "Godziny", after "Wskazania", with the sum of the hours of the schedule against the weekly norm of the person's FTE and the difference in words ("32 h z 40 h", "mniej o 8 h" or "równo z normą"), counted in the island after every click from the same optimistic list as the violations (`@src/components/hooks/useWorkload.ts`, `@src/lib/services/workload.ts`; nothing is requested or stored). The column is a `td` of its own, never part of the row header (`th scope="row"`) or of the `aria-label` of a shift button, because the smoke test compares those exactly.
- Decisions of the column: the weekly norm is 40 h times the FTE, less 8 h times the FTE for each holiday on a day other than Sunday among the seven days of the schedule (the norm of the `weekly_norm` warning); the sum is that of the full durations of the shifts that start in the schedule (a night shift on the last day counts in full); the norm is rounded to the minute to be shown and the difference is taken from the rounded value, while the `weekly_norm` rule compares exactly (with 3/7 of an FTE in a week without a holiday, 17 h 9 min of work reads "równo z normą" although the rule warns); there is no threshold, status or colour, because exceeding the norm is the warning of the list; absences are not counted until S-07 adds them and corrects the norm.
- Architecture and conventions: `@CLAUDE.md`.

## Commands

- `npm run dev` / `npm run build` / `npm run preview`: Cloudflare runtime; build needs both env vars.
- `npx astro sync`, `npm run lint`, `npx astro check`, `npm test`, `npm run build`: the CI gate, in this order.
- `node scripts/create-planner.mjs <email>`: registration is closed, so this creates the first planner or promotes an existing account. Needs `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, plus `PLANNER_PASSWORD` for a new account; it may target a hosted project, so check the host it prints. Pass the service-role key in the environment of that one command (the places the app itself may keep it are in Hard Rules).
- `npm run smoke`: auth-flow, role-matrix, closed-registration, employee-data, shift-data, schedule-data (tables, the `save_schedule` RPC, API, pages), assignment-data (the `schedule_assignments` table, the differential save and its `PT409` confirmation, the assignment routes, the assignment page) and invitation-flow (invite route, set-password page and route, activation time, locked e-mail) check against `BASE_URL` (default `http://localhost:4321`). It creates accounts through the admin API of a local Supabase, so it needs `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_KEY` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (from `supabase status -o env`; the app must read the same stack and, for the invitation route, the same service-role key in its `.dev.vars`), refuses any host except `127.0.0.1` / `localhost`, and must never run against a hosted project or the production Worker.
  - It also checks the live rules on a schedule of its own (`Smoke-<runId>-rules`, four employees, one for each scenario: a shift of 9 h alone, a break of 8 h, a break of exactly 11 h, seven days in a row, plus shortages), asserting the counter, the exact ordered list of reasons, the column "Wskazania" and the marks of the day cells; then it takes one assignment off through the route at a time and asserts that only what that assignment caused goes.
  - It also checks the column "Godziny" (the hours against the norm) on a schedule of its own (`Smoke-<runId>-hours`, the week from Tuesday 10.11.2026 with a holiday on Wednesday 11.11, so the norm of a full FTE is 32 h and of a half FTE 16 h; seven employees, one of them on 1/2 FTE), asserting the exact text of the column of each person (one shift, two shifts on different days, a night shift on the last day counted in full, nobody, a half FTE, exactly the norm and above it), the header, the paragraph under the table and that the column has no `role`, `aria-live`, icon or colour of a status; one assignment is taken off through the route at a time and only that person's text changes. The column of the API, route and rules schedules is asserted in the page steps that already read their rows.
- `npm test` (`npm run test:watch` to watch): Vitest 5 in workerd via `@cloudflare/vitest-plugin` (`@vitest.config.ts`), tests are `src/**/*.test.ts` next to the code, but never under `src/pages/` (Astro turns any `.ts` there into a route and would bundle vitest; test logic from `src/lib/`, or prefix the file with `_`). Labour-rule logic belongs in `src/lib/services/` as pure functions with no `astro:*` (does not resolve in tests) or `cloudflare:workers` imports; check "same input, same result" with `expectDeterministic` from `@src/lib/testing/determinism.ts`. Workerd in tests does not enforce the Workers Free 10 ms CPU limit.

## Coding Style

- ESLint type-checked (`@eslint.config.js`) and Prettier (`@.prettierrc.json`: width 120, double quotes); `no-console` warns outside `scripts/`.
- Use the `@/*` alias for `src/*` and `cn()` from `@/lib/utils` for class merging. No `"use client"` directives.
- Required by `@CLAUDE.md` for new code: React hooks in `src/components/hooks/`, and input validated with zod in one function shared by form and API route (`validateEmployeeInput` in `@src/lib/services/employee.ts`; `validateShiftInput` in `@src/lib/services/shift.ts` and `validateScheduleInput` in `@src/lib/services/schedule.ts` follow the same one-function rule with plain checks).

## Commit & Pull Request Guidelines

- Conventional Commits with a scope, as in history: `feat(redesign): ...`, `chore(deps): ...`, `docs(prd): ...`.
- Default branch is `master`; CI runs on pushes and PRs to it (`@.github/workflows/ci.yml`).
- `.husky/pre-commit` runs lint-staged but is inactive until `npx husky` is run (there is no `prepare` script).
